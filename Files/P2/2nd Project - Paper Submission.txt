2nd Project - Paper Submission
Requisitos de conclusão
2nd PROJECT: Submitting paper

Consider submitting a paper to CAMUL.

Context: 1st Project development 

This work is directly related to Project 1

Now, students must reflect on this practical experience and explore a related topic, with a focus on teaching or technology, in a scientific paper.

The papers should address subjects such as:

Technologies used or proposed in the project, such as Matterport or multimedia tools, and their application in education.
Pedagogical strategies developed or suggested for the MOOC.
Innovations or challenges identified in the creation and integration of the course in MOOC platforms.
Language: English

Template: The required template is available on the IEEE website. The Microsoft Word A4 template (.docx format) is recommended.

Length: The maximum length of the complete paper will be 6 pages with a minimum of 4 pages.

Structure: The paper must have the following structure:

1. Introduction

1.1. Conceptual or Theoretical Framework

1.2. Related Research

1.3. Study Objective

2. Method and Materials

2.1. Research Model

2.2. Methodology

2.3. Development

2.4. Data Collection Process

2.5. Data Analysis

3. Results

4. Discussion

5. Conclusion

6. Future Work

References (min 13 and <=5 years)

Presentation: The presentation will last 10 minutes.

Submission: 

The final work must be submitted as a .zip file, containing:
- The paper in Word and PDF formats.
- All graphs, tables, figures, photos and diagrams in separate files, with good resolution, numbered in the order in which they appear in the paper.
- The presentation in .ppt format.
